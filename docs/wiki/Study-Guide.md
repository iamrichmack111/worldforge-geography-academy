# Geography Study Guide

Use the 3D terrain lab to identify elevations, slopes, valleys and watersheds. Use Landform Quest for mountains and terrain types, and Earth Layers to explore Earth's crust, mantle, and core.

Practice continents, map-reading, landforms and climate. Assign worksheets in the homework area, then use quizzes and daily challenges for review.

The 3D world is simulated, not real Earth survey data.
