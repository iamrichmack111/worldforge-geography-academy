What this model teaches

Elevation, slope, watersheds, coastal landforms, climate, biomes, map layers, and vertical exaggeration.

Important: This is a synthetic world, not a real country or Earth elevation dataset. Elevation is scaled to illustrative metres. Biomes and rivers are simplified simulations, not a validated climate or hydrology model.

For authentic geography lessons, compare your findings with real topographic maps and satellite imagery.

WorldForge 3D uses a generated heightmap plus Three.js. No account or API key required. Browser must be online once to load the rendering library; all world data is served locally.
